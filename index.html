The method, in plain words

Full-to-full: a mileage segment runs between two consecutive FULL fills. Segment km/L = kilometres between them ÷ every litre poured after the previous FULL through this FULL (partial top-ups in between are folded in and the segment is labelled includes partial). It works because a full tank is the only repeatable reference point.

Its honest limits

  • km/L exists only between two FULL fills. A log of partials shows spend totals, but fuelbook refuses to guess mileage — it says "need two full fills to compute".
  • Entries before your first FULL or after your last FULL are excluded from km/L, with the reason shown on screen. They join after your next full fill.
  • "Full" varies with the pump's auto-cutoff, so single-tank figures wobble a few percent. Trust the lifetime line over any one tank.
  • All-in cost per km covers only the first-FULL-to-last-FULL window; expenses dated outside it are flagged and join after the next full fill.
  • The trend note is a prompt to check tyre pressure, service due, or fuel quality — never a mechanical diagnosis.
